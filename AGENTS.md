# Instructions for AI-assisted work

- Respect the stated scope of each task. Do not expand it into adjacent work.
- Identify important assumptions explicitly; do not present them as established facts.
- Work incrementally and leave meaningful decisions or direction changes at review checkpoints.
- Do not invent business facts or finalize open project decisions without direction.
- Avoid unrelated documentation, application code, and complexity that the task does not require.
- Consult the relevant project documents when needed; do not load every document for every task.
