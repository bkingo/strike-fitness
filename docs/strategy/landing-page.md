# Strike Fitness landing page strategy

**Status:** Approved Stage A strategy, with provisional direction and unresolved dependencies identified below. This document governs the landing page only; it does not specify design, campaign execution, technology, or enrollment implementation.

**Authority:** Use the [business document](strike-fitness.md) for membership benefits, prices, fees, terms, and Founders Rate details; the [audience strategy](audience.md) for hypothesized customer motivations and concerns; and the [decision log](../decisions.md) for approvals. The owner's subsequent Stage A decisions recorded here supersede earlier unresolved conversion direction, ranked-interest assumptions, and geographic proposals. Older proposal labels do not override recorded business approvals.

## 1. Purpose and conversion objectives

Strike Fitness is a fictional gym brand and portfolio project preparing for a local launch. The landing page's primary functional objective is lead generation through an intentional marketing email subscription.

The secondary journey is Founders Rate membership enrollment through a dedicated page, provisionally `/join`. Email subscription does not constitute enrollment, reserve promotional eligibility, or secure one of the 500 founding-member spots.

## 2. Subscriber value proposition and visitor journeys

Subscribers receive Founders Rate information, gym previews, and meaningful opening updates. Initial communications are general launch and promotional emails; personalized or interest-specific content is not part of the initial promise.

- Make launch-list signup prominent and easy to access, including from the hero area.
- Keep the Founders Rate CTA independently discoverable. Visitors can explore enrollment without subscribing, and subscribe without beginning enrollment.
- Associate the detailed membership comparison and eventual enrollment workflow primarily with `/join`. That page is intended for membership enrollment, rather than a second lead-capture experience.

Detailed enrollment functionality, checkout, and payment processing are outside the initial landing page scope. The interim membership CTA destination must be meaningful; its final behavior must be resolved before publication.

## 3. Content priorities and promotional boundaries

Lead with the gym's energy, atmosphere, and variety of workout opportunities while giving the Founders Rate meaningful promotional visibility. Showcase four broad categories: strength/cardio, general group fitness, specialty workouts, and amenities/recovery. Use audience motivations and concerns as hypotheses informing relevance, not verified customer findings.

Explain tier distinctions where needed: general classes are included in Plus and Complete; specialty classes are included in Complete. Amenity access varies, with sauna and Wi-Fi across all tiers and water-massage beds and tanning in Complete. Do not imply that Essential includes every activity or that separately purchased personal training is included in dues. Refer to the business document for full allocations and conditions.

Favor concise starting-price messaging: **Essential dues begin at $9.99/month**. A full three-tier comparison is not required on the landing page. Mandatory fees, applicable taxes, and relevant conditions must be accessible; starting dues must not appear to be an all-inclusive price.

Describe the Founders Rate using the approved business terms. Eligibility ends at the first **500 completed presale enrollments or club opening, whichever comes first**. Do not imply lifetime price protection or ongoing fee waivers. Do not invent remaining availability, fabricate spots-remaining counters, or introduce artificial countdowns.

Position the proposed club near **Hypoluxo Road and Jog Road**, serving the broader **Lake Worth / western Boynton Beach** area. No exact street address or opening date is approved. Do not invent operational details, conversion targets, market statistics, or additional incentives.

## 4. Lead-capture and consent requirements

**Approved fields:** First name and email address are required. Fitness interests are optional, with up to two selections of equal priority; there is no primary/secondary ranking. Exact interest choices and input presentation remain unresolved.

Signup must clearly communicate an intentional marketing email subscription and the promotional nature of communications. Provide accessible privacy information, support unsubscribing, and plan to record appropriate evidence of subscription consent.

**Provisional consent direction:** Use clear subscription disclosure associated with form submission rather than a separate marketing-consent checkbox. Final wording, privacy requirements, technical handling, and applicable compliance obligations require review before implementation. Determine how a published fictional portfolio project will handle real visitor data before enabling live collection or email sending.

## 5. Open decisions and later-stage dependencies

- **UX and creative:** Resolve precise CTA hierarchy, layout, and form presentation. Inline placement, a dedicated signup section, and a visitor-initiated dialog are possible; automatic signup popups are excluded from the initial experience. Final section order, wireframes, visual style, typography, colors, and components remain open. Explore presentation using real references and owner feedback in later creative and UX stages.
- **Conversion readiness:** Finalize fitness-interest choices and input presentation, resolve the meaningful interim enrollment destination before publication, and complete consent/privacy and real-data handling review before implementation or live collection as applicable.
- **Launch context:** Exact address and opening date remain unapproved. The broader pre-opening campaign is provisionally envisioned approximately 6–8 weeks before opening; no dates or email cadence are approved.

This strategy does not authorize a full marketing campaign, email sequence, technical architecture, brand guide, or enrollment specification.
