# Strike Fitness — proposed business offering

**Status:** Approved business decisions are recorded below for the three-tier structure and pricing, standard fees and membership terms, Founders Rate, and sauna/Wi-Fi access. Remaining operating details and launch assumptions are still proposals or open decisions. These fictional business policies are not legally validated membership terms and require appropriate legal review before use as an agreement.

**Established project decisions:** Strike Fitness is a fictional gym and portfolio project with its own brand identity. The owner’s Task 03B direction establishes close modeling of Crunch Lake Worth’s membership structure, facilities, and launch promotions, with realistic fees and conditions. Existing project records establish the broader project purpose; this document does not resolve landing page, design, technology, or integration decisions.

**Reference boundary:** “Crunch reference” means information documented in the completed [Lake Worth research](../research/crunch-lake-worth.md), checked October 9, 2026. Historical announcements establish advertised facilities and incentives, not proof of delivery. Current prices and benefits do not establish the original launch terms. All adaptations are proposed Strike decisions.

## 1. Business concept and positioning

**Proposal:** Strike Fitness is an affordable, full-service gym combining independent cardio and strength workouts, instructor-led classes, specialty training, paid coaching, and selected recovery amenities. A low-cost equipment membership provides an accessible starting point; higher tiers add distinct services rather than making every facility available at the entry price.

“Full-service” describes the club’s overall offering, not everything included in each membership. Monthly memberships are the core commercial model, with personal training sold separately. Affordability is a proposed position, not a verified local price comparison or profitability claim. Strike uses its own names and language without adopting Crunch slogans or branded programs.

## 2. Intended customer groups

**Proposed benefit groups, not verified demographics or campaign targeting:**

- Budget-conscious members seeking independent cardio and strength workouts.
- Members who prefer instructor-led group exercise and a regular class routine.
- Members seeking cycling, HIIT, heated classes, and recovery alongside equipment access.
- Beginners and experienced exercisers who may purchase individual coaching.

No age, income range, gender, commute radius, or dominant segment is established. Prioritizing any group requires owner approval.

All included classes require a place in the scheduled session; membership does not guarantee a reservation. Equipment-area access does not grant participation in coached HIIT sessions, and scheduled classes may temporarily occupy the functional area.

**Proposed guest conditions:** Guests register, provide identification, complete a waiver, and meet approved age requirements. Guest access covers equipment and standard facilities only; it excludes classes, recovery, and tanning. Propose local residents only, with no paid out-of-town guest program at launch. Exact residency and age rules remain open. The privilege allows one guest at a time, not unrestricted group entry.

All tiers serve one fictional club. Crunch’s current higher-tier multi-club access is a reference fact, but Strike has no approved network to promise. No coaching packages, training discounts, streaming, or standard membership merchandise are included. Separately sold services require disclosed prices and affirmative purchase; they are not automatic recurring charges.

## 5. Standard pricing, fees, and terms

**Crunch reference:** Current research records $75 standard enrollment, $59.99 recurring annual fees, and $2.25 monthly processing fees before applicable tax, plus a separate $10 signup annual-fee proration. It also records a $9.99 entry plan with a 12-month commitment and $24.99 / $29.99 higher plans with a 90-day minimum. The linked agreement contains renewal and cancellation ambiguities; Strike does not adopt that wording.

**Proposed Strike standard schedule, identical across tiers except dues and minimum term:**

- **Enrollment:** $75 once at signup, before applicable tax. This is additional to membership dues.
- **Monthly billing:** Dues plus $2.25 processing, before applicable tax: Essential **$12.24**, Plus **$27.24**, Complete **$32.24**. These are monthly charges, not all-inclusive prices; the annual fee is additional.
- **Annual fee:** $59.99 before applicable tax, first charged at the start of membership month 3 and every 12 months thereafter (months 15, 27, and so on). No annual fee is charged on or after an effective cancellation date; a paid annual fee is not prorated on ordinary cancellation, subject to applicable rights.
- **Activation:** Membership starts when the club opens for member workouts, or immediately for a post-opening signup. First monthly dues and processing are due at activation; later payments follow the monthly anniversary. Pre-opening signup collects enrollment only. A closing-day calendar convention must be specified for anniversaries falling on nonexistent dates.
- **Excluded charges:** No $10 signup proration, second annual fee, automatic coaching fee, or undisclosed facility surcharge. Proposed baseline has no late-payment, returned-payment, cancellation administration, or freeze fees. Any later fee proposal requires separate approval and disclosure.
- **Renewal:** All tiers renew monthly after their minimum term. Essential does not restart a new 12-month commitment automatically. A billing period means one monthly cycle, not 30 days; Plus and Complete use three cycles rather than copying an imprecise 90-day rule.
- **Ordinary cancellation:** Accept written notice electronically, in person, or by mail and acknowledge receipt. Cancellation takes effect 30 days after receipt, or at the end of the minimum term if later. Members may give notice during the minimum term. Scheduled monthly charges falling before the effective date remain payable; none are charged on or after that date. Already billed monthly periods are not prorated, and access continues through the paid period without further billing. Notice does not create a new minimum term or accelerate remaining dues into a lump-sum penalty.
- **Exceptions and changes:** Applicable cancellation/refund rights take precedence. Hardship, relocation, medical exceptions, minimum ages, payment authorization, and any pause policy need owner direction and review before operational use. No pause benefit is promised in this baseline. Propose no dues increases during the initial minimum term and at least 30 days’ advance notice of later price changes.
- **Pre-opening cancellation:** Refund all amounts collected if the member cancels before activation. If opening is delayed, activation and billing move with it; the member can cancel for a full refund. Do not start recurring billing merely because a forecast date passes.

Tax rates and taxable charges are not established here. Future prices must identify applicable tax and all mandatory fees without implying these proposed terms have legal approval.

## 6. Proposed Founders Rate promotion

**Historical reference:** Separate Crunch campaigns advertised $0 enrollment and annual savings, or $1 joining and a free first month; supplied screenshots also show merchandise and first-500 incentives. Complete terms and the basis of the annual savings are unknown. These offers are not proven to have stacked, and no lifetime rate guarantee is established.

**Recommended Strike proposal:** Offer the following package to new members on any tier who complete enrollment before the club opens, limited to the first **500 completed enrollments** or the opening, whichever comes first. This quantity is a proposed promotional limit, not facility capacity, a verified demand claim, or an approved commitment. No reservation alone qualifies, and cancellations do not replenish the allocation.

| Charge or benefit | Founders treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Signup enrollment | **$1 instead of $75**, before applicable tax. The $1 is the reduced enrollment fee, not an additional reservation charge. Enrollment is reduced by $74, not completely waived. |
| First month’s dues | Waived for the first complete monthly period from activation, for all tiers. |
| First month’s processing fee | Waived with that month’s dues. No other mandatory activation charge. |
| Later monthly charges | Normal dues plus $2.25 processing from month 2 onward, plus applicable tax. |
| First scheduled annual fee | The $59.99 fee normally due in month 3 is **waived**, not postponed or recovered later. |
| Later annual fees | Normal $59.99 before tax begins in month 15 and repeats annually while active. There is no lifetime annual-fee discount. |
| Welcome merchandise | One Strike T-shirt and one welcome bag per founding membership, collected at opening; sizing and contents require approval. |

**Payment example:** A Complete founder pays $1 plus applicable tax at presale signup, $0 at activation, then $32.24 plus applicable tax in month 2. Month 3 also costs $32.24 plus applicable tax because its annual fee is waived. Month 15 costs $92.23 plus applicable tax if the member is still active ($29.99 dues + $2.25 processing + $59.99 annual fee). Essential and Plus follow the same schedule at their own dues.

The initial free month counts toward the 12-period Essential minimum or three-period Plus/Complete minimum. Cancellation, refunds, and renewal otherwise follow section 5. Founders status does not lock dues for life, remove the minimum term, waive taxes on payable charges, or add access beyond the purchased tier.

Savings are **$74 enrollment + the selected tier’s first month dues + $2.25 processing + $59.99 on the first annual fee**, before tax, relative to the same standard membership remaining active through month 3. Annual-fee savings are one-time; do not claim “save $60 annually,” “zero enrollment,” or an all-inclusive $1 membership. The only signup charge is the $1 reduced enrollment fee plus applicable tax.

**Alternatives, not adopted:** Keep the $1 enrollment and first-month waiver but retain the normal annual fee; or offer $0 enrollment and a waived first annual fee while charging normal dues from activation. Each needs its own complete schedule and must replace, rather than silently stack with, the recommended offer.
