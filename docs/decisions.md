# Project decisions

This record contains established decisions only. Ideas under “Unresolved” are not approvals.

## Established

- **Project:** Strike Fitness is a fictional gym brand and portfolio project demonstrating frontend development and marketing technology skills.
- **Business reference:** The concept is inspired by the January 2025 opening of Crunch Fitness at 4681 Hypoluxo Rd, Lake Worth Beach, Florida. Crunch's facilities and launch promotion are references; Strike Fitness's identity, colors, and voice will be its own.
- **Landing page purpose and journeys (Stage A):** Approve lead generation as the primary functional objective, offering Founders Rate information, gym previews, and meaningful opening updates. Keep Founders Rate enrollment independently accessible through a dedicated page, provisionally `/join`; subscription does not enroll a member or reserve eligibility. **Rationale:** Support visitors at different levels of readiness without conflating interest with purchase. See the [landing page strategy](strategy/landing-page.md).
- **Priorities:** Visual quality is the primary goal; demonstrating relevant MarTech capabilities is secondary.
- **Working style:** Use an incremental, review-driven process with clear task scopes and explicit assumptions.
- **Membership structure and prices:** Approve the Crunch-inspired Strike Essential, Plus, and Complete tiers at $9.99, $24.99, and $29.99 monthly, respectively, with the documented tier benefit allocation. **Rationale:** Use the owner's approved close model of Crunch's three-tier structure while retaining Strike's own identity.
- **Standard fees and membership terms:** Approve the $75 enrollment fee, $2.25 monthly processing fee, and $59.99 annual fee on the documented schedule, plus the documented 12-period Essential and 3-period Plus/Complete minimums, monthly renewal, 30-day cancellation procedure, and pre-opening billing/refund rules. **Rationale:** Provide a clear, consistent fee schedule and membership commitment. These fictional policies remain subject to legal review.
- **Founders Rate:** Approve the $1 enrollment fee, first-month dues and processing waivers, waiver of the first scheduled annual fee, one T-shirt and welcome bag per founding membership, and eligibility through the first 500 completed presale enrollments or club opening, whichever comes first; normal recurring charges and terms then apply. **Rationale:** Combine the approved launch incentives while distinguishing one-time savings from ongoing charges.
- **Sauna and Wi-Fi:** Include sauna access and Wi-Fi for members of Essential, Plus, and Complete. **Rationale:** Adopt the approved Crunch-inspired amenity allocation across all three tiers.
- **Landing page content and geography (Stage A):** Approve experience-led promotion of strength/cardio, general group fitness, specialty workouts, and amenities/recovery, with meaningful Founders Rate visibility, accurate tier distinctions, and concise starting-dues messaging with accessible fees and conditions. Position the proposed club near Hypoluxo Road and Jog Road for the broader Lake Worth / western Boynton Beach area; no exact address or opening date is approved. **Rationale:** Communicate local relevance and workout variety while keeping costs, access, and promotional eligibility accurate.
- **Launch-list capture and consent (Stage A):** Require first name and email; allow up to two optional, equally ranked fitness interests, superseding primary/secondary ranking. Promise general launch and promotional communications initially. Provisionally use submission-associated subscription disclosure rather than a separate consent checkbox, subject to review; exclude automatic signup popups. **Rationale:** Keep subscription intentional and straightforward without promising personalization or finalizing consent and real-data handling requirements.
