# Strike Fitness

Strike Fitness is a fictional gym brand and portfolio project for demonstrating frontend development and marketing technology skills. It is inspired by the January 2025 opening of Crunch Fitness at 4681 Hypoluxo Rd, Lake Worth Beach, Florida.

The project is intended to become a polished, responsive launch landing page that attracts prospective members, captures email addresses, and collects primary and secondary fitness interests for possible email marketing and segmentation. Strike Fitness will have its own visual identity, colors, and brand voice.

## Established requirements

- The primary project goal is a visually excellent landing page; the secondary goal is to demonstrate relevant MarTech skills.
- The intended page is responsive and supports email and fitness-interest capture.
- Crunch Fitness's Lake Worth location and launch promotion are business references, not Strike Fitness facts or branding.
