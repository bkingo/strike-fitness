# Strike Fitness

Strike Fitness is a fictional fitness company established in 2026 for a portfolio project. The project will demonstrate an end-to-end marketing technology workflow:

1. Research
2. Business strategy
3. Brand positioning
4. Landing page strategy
5. Landing page
6. Lead capture
7. Email service provider (ESP) integration
8. Segmentation
9. Email lifecycle
10. Analytics
11. Optimization

The current phase establishes a clean foundation for research, strategy, and design documentation. Application development and marketing technology implementation will come later.

Research into Crunch Fitness will inform the project's competitive and market analysis. Strike Fitness is not a clone of Crunch Fitness: it will have its own positioning, audience, offer, and brand identity, all of which will be defined through future research and strategy work.
